## Definition

A two-player game has a **value** if $\underline{v} = \overline{v}$. The quantity $v := \underline{v} = \overline{v}$ is called the **value of the game**. Any maxmin and minmax strategies of P1 and P2 respectively are then called _optimal strategies_.

(convention: P1 maximizer, P2 minimizer)

## Notes

The [[von Neumann's minimax theorem|minimax theorem]] relates when values may be found for [[mixed strategy|mixed strategies]].
