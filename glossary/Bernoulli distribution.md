## Definition

$X$ [[random variable]] with Bernoulli distribution, then
$$\Pr(X = 1) = p=1-P(X=0)=1-q$$

## probability density function

$$f_n(k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1 - p)^{n-k}, \quad k \in \{0, 1, \ldots, n\}$$
