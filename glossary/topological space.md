## Definition

A **topological space** $(X,\tau)$ is a [[set]] $X$ equipped with a [[topology]] $\tau$.

## Notes

- _topology_ is closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections, whereas [[measurable space]] is closed under countable unions, countable intersections, and complements
- a [[Borel σ-algebra]] is generated by _open sets_ of a _topological space_
- more abstractly, a _topological space_ is a type of _structure_ on a _set_
- the [[category]], _Top_ has _topological spaces_ as _objects_ and _continuous functions_ as its _morphisms_
