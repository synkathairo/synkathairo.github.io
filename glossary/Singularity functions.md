define
$$\mu_{i+1} = \int_{-\infty}^t \mu_i(\lambda - t) d\lambda$$
where $\mu_0(t) = \delta(t)$, i.e. [[delta function|δ-function]] or unit impulse,

and, $\mu_1(t) = 1(t)$, i.e. [[Heaviside step function|Step function]]
