## Theorem (Aumann, 1976)

Let $(N, Y, (\mathcal{F}_i)_{i \in N}, \mathfrak{s}, \mathbf{P})$ be an [[Aumann model of incomplete information with beliefs]], and suppose $n=2$ (i.e. 2 players). Let $A \subseteq Y$ be an [[Aumann model of incomplete information#Definition (event)|event]] and let $\omega \in Y$ be a state of the world. If the event "Player I ascribes probability $q_\textrm{I}$ to $A$" is [[common knowledge]] in $\omega$, and the event "Player II ascribes probability $q_\textrm{II}$ to $A$" is also common knowledge in $\omega$, then $q_\textrm{I} = q_\textrm{II}$.
