## Definition (SI, 2022)

The **Boltzmann constant** is defined as $k = 1.380649 \times 10^{-23} \, \text{J} \cdot \text{K}^{-1}$.

(a _fundamental physical constant_ in the system of _SI units_, i.e. the "metric system")

## Notes

- also denoted as $k_B$
- The _Boltzmann constant_ defines temperature (in _kelvins_) in terms of _joules_, viz. every $1\, \text{K}$ change of _thermodynamic temperature_ corresponds to a _thermal energy_ change of exactly $1.380649 \times 10^{-23} \, \text{J}$
- In the _statistical definition of entropy_, where $W$ refers to the number of distinct microscopic states available to the system given macroscopic constraints,
  - $S = k \ln W$
  - $k$ therefore scales the value of entropy by physical units
  - this definition of entropy is related to [[entropy of a random variable|information entropy]] via _Gibbs entropy_
