## Definition

If $X$ is a [[topological space]] and $S \subseteq X$ is an arbitrary _subset_, define the **subspace topology** on $S$ via declaring _subset_ $U \subseteq S$ to be [[open set|open]] in $S$ if and only if there exists an _open subset_ $V \subseteq X$ such that $U = V \cap S$.

A subset of $S$ that is [[open set|open]] or [[closed set|closed]] in this _subset topology_ said to be _relatively open_ or _relatively closed_ in $S$.

Any subset of $X$ endowed with the _subspace topology_ is said to be a **subspace**.
