## Definition

The **Karhunen-Loève transform** (KLT) is an _orthogonal transform_ in form $\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{V}^T \mathbf{x}$ where orthogonal transform matrix $\mathbf{V} = [\mathbf{v}_1,...,\mathbf{v}_d]$ is the [[eigenvalue and eigenvector|eigenvector]] matrix of [[covariance]] matrix $\mathbf{\Sigma}_x$ of $\mathbf{x}$, composed of $d$ normalized eigenvectors of $\mathbf{\Sigma}_x$.
