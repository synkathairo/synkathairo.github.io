## Definition

A **mixed strategy** for a player is a probability distribution on the space of his [[pure strategy|pure strategies]]. Equivalently, a [[random variable]] whose values are the player's pure strategies.

Define

- $\Sigma_i$, player $i$'s set of mixed strategies
- $\mathcal{B}_i$, player $i$'s set of behavior strategies

## Theorem (convexity and compactness of mixed strategies for finite pure strategy set)

If player $i$'s set of pure strategies $S_i$ is finite, then his set of mixed strategies $\Sigma_i$ is [[convex set|convex]] and [[compact set|compact]].
