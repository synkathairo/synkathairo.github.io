## Definition

The **correlation coefficient** is defined as
$$\rho_{XY} = \frac{C_{XY}}{\sigma_X \sigma_Y}$$
where $C_{XY}$ is the [[covariance]] of $X$ and $Y$ and $\sigma_X$, $\sigma_Y$ are the _standard deviations_ of $X$ and $Y$ respectively (whereas $\sigma_X^2$, $\sigma_Y^2$ would be the [[variance|variances]]).
