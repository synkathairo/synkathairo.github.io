## Definition
A **Banach space** is a [[complete metric space|complete]] [[vector space]] $B$ with a [[norm]] $\lVert \cdot \rVert$ 

(i.e., a *normed vector space* $B$ where every [[Cauchy sequence]] in $B$ converges)

## Notes
- [[Lp space]] is a typical example of a *Banach space*
