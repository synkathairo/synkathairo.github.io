## Definition

A _subset_ $H \subseteq G$ is a **subgroup** of [[group]] $G$ if the following properties hold:

- _closure_, if $a \in H$, $b\in H$, then $ab \in H$
- _identity_, $1 \in H$
- _inverses_, if $a \in H$, then $a^{-1}\in H$
