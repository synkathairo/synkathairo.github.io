## Theorem

$\mathbb{E}[X+Y] = \mathbb{E}[X]+\mathbb{E}[Y]$

where $E[X]$ is [[expectation]] of $X$

## Notes

- always true, no assumptions needed
- also applies to integral? when certain conditions apply (think of how expectation is an integral)
