The Goldman-Tucker theorem establishes strict complementary slackness conditions for [[linear programming|linear programs]], if there is a finite-valued optimal solution, then,

$\exists x^*, \lambda^*$ with strict complementarity (only one of them zero) holding, i.e. not both of $x^*, \lambda^*$ are zero.
