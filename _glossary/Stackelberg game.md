## Definition

One player is the *leader*, selecting an initial [[strategy]], and another player is the *follower*, who chooses *strategy* in response to the strategy of the leader (hence also known as *leader-follower game*)

in game matrix, e.g. leader chooses row, and follower chooses a column based on the choices in that row

admits a [[Stackelberg equilibrium]]

## Notes

- Stackelberg game is a special case of [[extensive form game]]
