## Thesis

Claim: any real-world computation can be translated into an equivalent computation involving a [[Turing machine]]

i.e. any real-world computation can be done using lambda calculus ie. _general recursive functions_

alternatively,

1. All reasonable formalizations of the intuitive notion of [[effectively computable|effective computability]] are equivalent.
2. [[Turing machine]] _computability_ is a reasonable formalization of _effective computability_.

(notions of effective procedure: [[Turing machine]], _recursive functions_, [[lambda calculus]], [[random access machine]], _Markov algorithms_)
