## Proposition
  
- $E\left\lbrack E\left\lbrack Y|X \right\rbrack \right\rbrack = E\lbrack Y\rbrack$
- $E\left\lbrack E\left\lbrack g(X,Y)~|~X \right\rbrack \right\rbrack = E\left\lbrack g(X,Y) \right\rbrack$
