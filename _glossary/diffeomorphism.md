## Definition
A **diffeomorphism** is a [[smooth function|smooth]] map $f: U \to V$ where $f$ is [[injective function|one-to-one]] and [[surjective|onto]] (i.e. [[surjective|one-to-one correspondence]]), and $f^{-1}: V \to U$ is also *smooth*.
