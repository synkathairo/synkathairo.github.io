## Theorem (bound for $x > 55$)

Let $\pi(x)$ denote the number of [[prime number|primes]] less than some integer $x$. For $x>55$,
$$\frac{x}{\log x} \leq \pi(x)\leq \frac{x}{\log x - 4}$$
