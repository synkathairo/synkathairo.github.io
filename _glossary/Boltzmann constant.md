## Definition (SI, 2022)

The **Boltzmann constant** is defined as $k = 1.380649 \times 10^{-23} \, \text{J} \cdot \text{K}^{-1}$.

(a *fundamental physical constant* in the system of *SI units*, i.e. the "metric system")

## Notes

- also denoted as $k_B$
- The *Boltzmann constant* defines temperature (in *kelvins*) in terms of *joules*, viz. every $1\, \text{K}$ change of *thermodynamic temperature* corresponds to a *thermal energy* change of exactly $1.380649 \times 10^{-23} \, \text{J}$
- In the *statistical definition of entropy*, where $W$ refers to the number of distinct microscopic states available to the system given macroscopic constraints,
	- $S = k \ln W$
	- $k$ therefore scales the value of entropy by physical units
	- this definition of entropy is related to [[entropy of a random variable|information entropy]] via *Gibbs entropy*
