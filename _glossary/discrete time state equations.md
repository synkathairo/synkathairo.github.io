[[State equation of finite dimensional linear systems]]

## Definition

$$ \begin{aligned}
\mathbf{x}(t_{k+1}) = A(t_k) \mathbf{x}(t_k) + B(t_k) \mathbf{u}(t_k) \\
\mathbf{y}(t_{k}) = C(t_k) \mathbf{x}(t_k) + D(t_k) \mathbf{u}(t_k)
\end{aligned}$$
$$
