## Definition

A solution concept is a function that associates every _bargaining game_ with a proposed agreement.

A single-valued **solution concept** $\varphi$ is a functional associating every [[coalitional game]] $(N;v)$ and every _coalitional structure_ $\mathcal{B}$ with an imputation $\varphi(N; v; \mathcal{B}) \in \mathbb{R}^N$
