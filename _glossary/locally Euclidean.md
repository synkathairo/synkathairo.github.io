## Definition

Let $X$ be a [[topological space]], then $X$ is called **locally Euclidean** of dimension $n$ if for every $x \in X$ there exists a _neighborhood_ $U \subseteq X$, a $V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, and a [[homeomorphism]] $\phi: U \to V$.

Then, the triple $(\varphi,U,V)$ is called a **chart** (around $p$).
