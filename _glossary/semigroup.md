## Definition

A **semigroup** is a mathematical object defined for a [[set]] and a *binary operator* in which the multiplication operation is *associative*.

## Notes

- special case of *magma* where the operation is *associative*
- generalization of [[group]] without requiring *identity element* or *inverse*
