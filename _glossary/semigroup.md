## Definition

A **semigroup** is a mathematical object defined for a [[set]] and a _binary operator_ in which the multiplication operation is _associative_.

## Notes

- special case of _magma_ where the operation is _associative_
- generalization of [[group]] without requiring _identity element_ or _inverse_
