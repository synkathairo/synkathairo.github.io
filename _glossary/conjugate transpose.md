## Definition

The **conjugate transpose** (i.e. **Hermitian transpose**, **adjoint matrix**) $M^*$ of a matrix $M$ is the _transpose_ of the matrix $M$ where each entry is replaced by its _complex conjugate_, i.e. complex number $a + ib$ becomes $a - ib$ and vice versa.

(note for real matrix, the _conjugate transpose_ simply yields the _transpose matrix_)

A **Hermitian matrix** (or **self-adjoint matrix**) is one satisfying $M = M^*$ (generalization of _symmetric matrix_ for real matrices).
