## Definition

Given $A \subset \mathbb{R}^n$, the [[closed set]] $Y \subset \mathbb{R}^k$, the [[ correspondence]] $f: A \to Y$ is **upper hemicontinuous** (u.h.c.) if it has a [[closed graph]] and the images of [[compact set|compact sets]] are bounded, i.e. for every compact $B \subset A$, the set $f(B) = \{y \in Y: y \in f(x) \text{ for some } x \in B\}$ is bounded.

## Notes

A more general form exists in [[upper semicontinuous function|upper semicontinuous functions]] dealing with relations or correspondences in general

$f$ is a set-valued function here
