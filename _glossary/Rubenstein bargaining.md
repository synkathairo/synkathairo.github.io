## Definition

Two players agree to share a pie of size $1$. Player 1 proposes sharing rule $(x,1-x)$ in periods $0, 2, 4$ etc. that player 2 can accept or reject. Game ends if a player accepts. If player 2 rejects player 1's offer in period $2k$, in period $2k+1$ can propose sharing rule $(x,1-x)$ that player 1 can accept or reject.

This is an [[infinite-horizon game]] of [[game with perfect information|perfect information]].
