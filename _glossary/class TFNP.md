## Definition
Define the *complexity class*, **TFNP** (*total functions from NP*), as all [[total relation|total]] [[search problem|search problems]] in [[class FNP|FNP]].

## Notes
- could also be called $\mathrm{F}(\mathrm{NP} \cap \mathrm{coNP})$
