## Diagonalizable matrix
Consider a matrix $A$ that may be diagonalized. Then, there exists a nonsingular matrix $T$ such that $T^{-1} A T = \Lambda$ where $\Lambda = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1...\lambda_n)$.
