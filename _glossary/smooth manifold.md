## Definition
An $n$-dimensional **smooth manifold**, $(M, \mathcal{A})$, is an $n$-dimensional [[topological manifold]] $M$, equipped with an equivalence class of [[atlas]] $\mathcal{A}$, called its *smooth structure*.
