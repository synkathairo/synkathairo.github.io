If $\mathcal{S}$ is [[convex set|convex]], then for any $\mathbf{y} \in \mathcal{S}$,
$$||\mathbf{y}-\mathsf{P}_\mathcal{S}(\mathbf{x})||_2 \leq ||\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{x}||_2$$
