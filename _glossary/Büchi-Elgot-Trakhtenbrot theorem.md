## Theorem (_Büchi-Elgot-Trakhtenbrot_)

A [[decision problem|language]] ($L$) of _finite_ [[word|words]] is definable in [[monadic second-order logic|monadic logic]] (i.e. the set of its structures $K_L$ is definable in $MSOL$) iff it is [[regular language|regular]]

Furthermore the _translations_ are [[effectively computable|effective]] and thus _satisfiability_ of _monadic logic_ over _finite words_ is [[effectively decidable|decidable]]

(the [[theory|theory]] of _weak_ [[monadic second-order logic]] over $(\mathbb{N},<)$ is [[effectively decidable|decidable]], where _weak_ means that second-order variables refer to [[finite set|finite sets]])
