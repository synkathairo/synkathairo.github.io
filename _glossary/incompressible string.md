## Definition
A *string* $x$ is **incompressible** or **Kolmogorov random** if $K(x) \geq \lvert x \rvert$. 
(where $K(x)$ is [[Kolmogorov complexity]])

## Theorem
It is *undecidable* whether a *string* is *incompressible*.
