## Definition

A _string_ $x$ is **incompressible** or **Kolmogorov random** if $K(x) \geq \lvert x \rvert$.
(where $K(x)$ is [[Kolmogorov complexity]])

## Theorem

It is _undecidable_ whether a _string_ is _incompressible_.
