## Definition

Suppose $A_1,A_2,.. \in \mathcal{F}$, where $\mathcal{F}$ is a [[σ-algebra|field]], then it is a **Borel field** if all unions and intersections are also in $\mathcal{F}$.

Alternatively: the **Borel σ-algebra** is the [[σ-algebra]] generated by the [[open set|open sets]].

## Notes

- the _open sets_ of a [[topological space]] $(X,\mathcal{F})$ general a [[σ-algebra]] which is the _Borel σ-algebra_ of the space
