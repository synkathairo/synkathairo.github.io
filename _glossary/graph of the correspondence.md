## Definition
Given $A \subset \mathbb{R}^N$ and $Y \subset \mathbb{R}^K$, the **graph of the correspondence** $f : A \mapsto Y$ is the set
$$\{(x,y) \in A \times Y : y \in f(x)\}$$
