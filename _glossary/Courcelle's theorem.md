## Theorem (_Courcelle's theorem_)

Let $k \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\varphi$ be an [[monadic second-order logic|mso]] _formula_ over the _vocabulary_ of _graphs_, i.e. using a _binary relation_ $E(x,y)$. There is a _cubic algorithm_ which inputs a [[undirected graph|undirected graphs]] and fails or answers if the graph satisfies $\varphi$. The algorithm succeeeds if the graph has [[treewidth]] $\leq k$
