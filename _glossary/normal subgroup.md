## Definition
Suppose $H$ is a [[subgroup]] of [[group]] $G$, then, $H$ is a **normal subgroup** if for all $h$ in $H$, all $g$ in $G$, $ghg^{-1}$ is in $H$.

## Proposition
above definition is equivalent to the following conditions:
- for all $g$ in $G$, $gHg^{-1} = H$
- for all $g$ in $G$, [[coset|left coset]] $gH$ is equal to *right coset* $Hg$
- every *left coset* of $H$ in $G$ is a *right coset*
