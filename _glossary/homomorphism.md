## Definition
Suppose [[group|groups]] $G$ and $G'$, then a **homomorphism** is a [[function|map]] $\varphi: G \to G'$ such that for all $a,b \in G$, $$\varphi(ab)=\varphi(a)\varphi(b)$$

## Notes
- not to be confused with *homeomorphism*
- see [[group isomorphism|isomorphism]], which includes an additional requirement of bijection
