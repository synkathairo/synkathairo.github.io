## Definition

Suppose $P$ is a finite [[partial order|partially ordered set]]. Then a **chain** in $P$ is a set of pairwise comparable elements (i.e. [[total order|totally ordered]] subset)

## Notes

- a *chain* in $S$ is subset $A \hookrightarrow S$ such that $\leq$ is a *total order* on $A$
