## Theorem

Let $f : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [[continuous function]] defined on a *nonempty* and [[compact set]] $\Omega$. Then, there exists a *minimizer* $x^* \in \Omega$ of $f$ on $\Omega$, that is $$f(x^*) \leq f(x) \quad \forall x \in \Omega$$
