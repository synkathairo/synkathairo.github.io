## Definition
Define **indifference relation** $\approx_i$, which expresses the fact that a player is indifferent between two possible outcomes
$$x \approx_i y \iff x \succsim_i y \text{ and } y \succsim_i x$$
where $\succsim_i$ denotes the [[preference relation|preference relation]].
