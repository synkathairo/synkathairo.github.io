## Definition
Suppoose a [[normal subgroup]] $N$ of a [[group]] $N$, then, the set of [[coset|cosets]] of $N$ is a *group*, called the **quotient group**, dennoted as $G/N$ 

## Notes
- the elements of the quotient group are actually sets, or rather, they are cosets, rather than individual elements of $G$; 
	- indeed, a [[law of composition]] may be defined on the set of cosets of a normal subgroup $N$ of any group $G$
