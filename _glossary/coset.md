## Definition

Suppose a [[group]] $G$, with [[subgroup]] $H$, then, a **left coset** $xH = \{xh : h \in H\}$ for an element $a$ in $G$. Similarly, a **right coset** may take the form $Ha$.

## Notes
- cosets of $H$ in $G$ are equivalence classes for congruence relation,
	- $a \equiv b$ if $b=ah$ for some $h$ in $H$
