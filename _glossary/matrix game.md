## Defintion
A type of [[two-player zero-sum game|two-person zero-sum game]], where there are two players, player 1 (P1) and player 2 (P2) and a ($m \times n$)-dimensional (real-valued) matrix $A = \{a_{ij}\}$, each entry denoting an outcome of the game. For P1, alternatives are the $m$ rows of the game, for P2 possible choices are the $n$ columns of the same matrix.

(note: convention that P1 is minimizer, P2 is maximizer, different in some textbooks)
