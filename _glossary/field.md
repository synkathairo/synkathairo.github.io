## Definition
Define a **field** $F$ as a [[set]] together with two [[law of composition|laws of composition]], 
- $F \times F \stackrel{+}\to F$, *addition*: $a,b \leadsto a + b$ 
- $F \times F \stackrel{\times}\to F$, *multiplication*: $a, b \leadsto ab$
such that the axioms below are satisfied,
- addition makes $F$ into an *abelian* [[group]] $F^+$ with identity element denoted $0$
- multiplication is commutative and makes the set of nonzero elements of $F$ into an *abelian group* $F^\times$, with its identity element denoted $1$
- *distributive law*, for all $a,b,c \in F$, $a (b+c) = ab + ac$

## Notes
- not to be confused with [[σ-algebra]] from analysis (which is also known as a "field")
