## Definition

**fixed parameter tractable** (FPT) problems, class of [[parameterized problem|parameterized problems]] that can be solved in $f(k) \cdot n^{O(1)}$ time

## Notes

- also known as *para-P* (compare to [[class P|P]])
- analogous to [[polynomial-time Karp reducible|Karp reduction]], there is also [[FPT-reducibility|FPT-reduction]]
