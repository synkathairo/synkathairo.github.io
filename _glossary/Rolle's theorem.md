## Theorem

Suppose *real-valued function* $f$ is [[continuous function|continuous]] on proper *closed interval* $[a,b]$, [[differentiable function|differentiable]] on *open interval* $(a,b)$, and $f(a) = f(b)$, then there exists at least one $c \in (a,b)$ such that $f'(c) = 0$ (i.e. $f$ has a critical point in $(a,b)$).

## Notes

- may be considered a special case of [[mean value theorem]]
- see [[Taylor's theorem]]
