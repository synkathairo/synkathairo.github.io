## Theorem

Suppose _real-valued function_ $f$ is [[continuous function|continuous]] on proper _closed interval_ $[a,b]$, [[differentiable function|differentiable]] on _open interval_ $(a,b)$, and $f(a) = f(b)$, then there exists at least one $c \in (a,b)$ such that $f'(c) = 0$ (i.e. $f$ has a critical point in $(a,b)$).

## Notes

- may be considered a special case of [[mean value theorem]]
- see [[Taylor's theorem]]
