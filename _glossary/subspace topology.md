## Definition
If $X$ is a [[topological space]] and $S \subseteq X$ is an arbitrary *subset*, define the **subspace topology** on $S$ via declaring *subset* $U \subseteq S$ to be [[open set|open]] in $S$ if and only if there exists an *open subset* $V \subseteq X$ such that $U = V \cap S$.

A subset of $S$ that is [[open set|open]] or [[closed set|closed]] in this *subset topology* said to be *relatively open* or *relatively closed* in $S$.

Any subset of $X$ endowed with the *subspace topology* is said to be a **subspace**.
