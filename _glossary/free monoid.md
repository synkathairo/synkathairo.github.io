## Definition

A **free monoid** on a [[set]] is the [[monoid]] whose elements are _finite sequences_ (or [[word|strings]]) of zero or more elements from the _set_, with _string concatenation_ as the _monoid operator_ and the _empty string_ (sequence of zero elements) as the _identity element_.
