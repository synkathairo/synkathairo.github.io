## Definition

A **free monoid** on a [[set]] is the [[monoid]] whose elements are *finite sequences* (or [[word|strings]]) of zero or more elements from the *set*, with *string concatenation* as the *monoid operator* and the *empty string* (sequence of zero elements) as the *identity element*.
