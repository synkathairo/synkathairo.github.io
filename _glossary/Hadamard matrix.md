## Definition
A **Hadamard matrix** is a square matrix that has entries of $-1$ or $+1$, with mutually orthogonal rows.

## Sylvester's construction
For order $n$ *Hadamard matrix*, may construct order $2n$ *Hadamard matrix* by 
$$\begin{bmatrix} H & H \\ H & -H \end{bmatrix}$$
