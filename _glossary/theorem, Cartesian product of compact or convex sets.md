## Theorem

If $A \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ and $B \subset \mathbb{R}^m$ are _compact sets_, then the set $A \times B$ is a [[compact set|compact subset]] of $\mathbb{R}^{n + m}$. If $A$ and $B$ are [[convex set|convex sets]], then $A \times B$ is a convex subset of $\mathbb{R}^{n + m}$.
