## Definition

A **parameterized problem** is a [[decision problem|language]] $L \subseteq \Sigma^* \times \mathbb{N}$, where $\Sigma$ is a finite, fixed *alphabet*. For an instance $(x,k) \in \Sigma^* \times \mathbb{N}$, refer to $k$ as the *parameter*.
