## Definition

A (group) **isomorphism** $\varphi: G \to G'$ is a [[bijective function|bijective]] [[group]] [[homomorphism]] i.e. _bijective map_ s.t. $\varphi(a,b) = \varphi(a) \varphi(b)$ for all $a,b\in G$.

## Lemma

If $\varphi : G \to G'$ is an _isomorphism_, then the _inverse map_ $\varphi^{-1}: G' \to G$ is also an _isomorphism_.

## Definition

Two _groups_ $G$ and $G'$ are said to be **isomorphic** if there exists an _isomorphism_ $\varphi$ between the two groups; this may be indicated as $G \approx G'$.
