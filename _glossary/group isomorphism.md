## Definition
A (group) **isomorphism** $\varphi: G \to G'$ is a [[bijective function|bijective]] [[group]] [[homomorphism]] i.e. *bijective map* s.t. $\varphi(a,b) = \varphi(a) \varphi(b)$ for all $a,b\in G$.

## Lemma
If $\varphi : G \to G'$ is an *isomorphism*, then the *inverse map* $\varphi^{-1}: G' \to G$ is also an *isomorphism*.

## Definition
Two *groups* $G$ and $G'$ are said to be **isomorphic** if there exists an *isomorphism* $\varphi$ between the two groups; this may be indicated as $G \approx G'$.
