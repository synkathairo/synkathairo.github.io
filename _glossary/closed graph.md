## Definition

Given $A \subset \mathbb{R}^N$, the [[closed set]] $Y \subset \mathbb{R}^K$, the [[correspondence]] $f: A \to Y$ has a **closed graph** if for any 2 sequence $x^m \to x \in A$ and $y^m \to y$ with $x^m \in A$ and $y^m \in f(x^m)$ for every $m$, we have $y \in f(x)$.

### Notes

- $f$ is a set-valued function here
- this describes a relationship between two [[topological space|spaces]]
