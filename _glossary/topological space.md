## Definition
A **topological space** $(X,\tau)$ is a [[set]] $X$ equipped with a [[topology]] $\tau$.

## Notes
- *topology* is closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections, whereas [[measurable space]] is closed under countable unions, countable intersections, and complements
- a [[Borel σ-algebra]] is generated by *open sets* of a *topological space*
- more abstractly, a *topological space* is a type of *structure* on a *set*
- the [[category]], *Top* has *topological spaces* as *objects* and *continuous functions* as its *morphisms*
