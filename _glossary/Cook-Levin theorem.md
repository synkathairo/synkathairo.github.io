## Theorem

1. [[SAT problem|SAT]] is [[class NP-complete|NP-complete]]
2. [[k-SAT problem|3SAT]] is [[class NP-complete|NP-complete]]

## Notes

- NP-complete Boolean satisfiability problem
- 2SAT is in [[class P|P]]
- [[k-SAT problem|kSAT]] is [[class NP-complete|NP-complete]] for $k \geq 3$
