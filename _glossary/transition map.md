## Definition
If $(U,\varphi)$, $(V,\psi)$ are two [[locally Euclidean|charts]] such that $U \cap V \neq \emptyset$, *composite map* $\psi \circ \varphi^{-1}: \varphi(U \cap V) \to \psi(U \cap V)$ is called a **transition map**.
