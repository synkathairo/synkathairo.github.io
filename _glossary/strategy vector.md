## Definition (mixed/behavior strategy vector)

A **mixed/behavior strategy vector** is a vector of strategies $\sigma = (\sigma_i)_i$ in which $\sigma_i$ can be either a mixed strategy or a behavior strategy of player $i$, for each $i$.
