## Definition

Consider a hypothetical [[probability density function|probability distribution]] $Q$ and true probability distribution $P$ on the same sample space. Then, the **Kullback-Leibler divergence** (_KL divergence_ or _relative entropy_),

$${\displaystyle D_{\text{KL}}(P\parallel Q)=\sum _{x\in {\mathcal {X}}}P(x)\,\log {\frac {P(x)}{Q(x)}}{\text{.}}}$$
