## Definition
Let $(S, \mathcal{O})$ be a [[topological space]] and let $f : S \longrightarrow \hat{\mathbb{R}}$ be a [[function]]. Its **epigraph** is the set
$$\mathrm{epi}(f) = \{(x,y) \in S \times \mathbb{R} \mid f(x) \leq y\}$$

The **hypograph** of $f$ is the set
$$\mathrm{hyp}(f) = \{(x,y) \in S \times \mathbb{R} \mid y \leq f(x)\}$$
