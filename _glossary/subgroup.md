## Definition

A *subset* $H \subseteq G$ is a **subgroup** of [[group]] $G$ if the following properties hold:
- *closure*, if $a \in H$, $b\in H$, then $ab \in H$
- *identity*, $1 \in H$
- *inverses*, if $a \in H$, then $a^{-1}\in H$
