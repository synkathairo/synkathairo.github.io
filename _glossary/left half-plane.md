## Definition
portion of [[complex numbers|complex]] plane $z = x+iy$ with [[real numbers|real]] part $\mathfrak{R}[z] < 0$ 

## Notes
Consider the plot with vertical imaginary axis vs horizontal real axis, then the left side of this plot is the left half-plane.
