Let $Z$ be a Chi-squared random variable with $k$ degrees of freedom.
$$\mathrm{Pr}[|\mathbb{E}Z-Z| \geq \epsilon\mathbb{E}Z] \leq 2 e^{-k\epsilon^2/8}$$
