LFor a fixed symmetric matrix $A$, the normalized quadratic form
$$\frac{x^T A x}{x^T x}$$
is called a **Rayleigh quotient**.

Given a positive definite matrix $B$ of the same size, the quantity
$$\frac{x^T A x}{x^T Bx}$$
is called a **generalized Rayleigh quotient**.
