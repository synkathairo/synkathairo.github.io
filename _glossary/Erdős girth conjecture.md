## Conjecture

For every $k \geq 1$, there exists an $n$-node graph with $\Omega(n^{1+1/k})$ edges and _girth_ at least $2k+2$.

(where _girth_ refers to the length of the shortest cycle contained in the graph)

(unproven?)
