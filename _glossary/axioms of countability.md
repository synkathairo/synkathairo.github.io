## Definition
### A1 (first-countable)
A **first-countable** [[topological space]] $(X, \mathcal{T})$, (i.e. $A_1$*-space*) is one with a [[countable|countable]] *neighborhood basis*, i.e. 
- for any $x \in X$, there exists a *countable* family of [[open set|open]] *neighborhoods* of $x$, $\{U_1^x, U_2^x, U_3^x, ...\}$, such that for any $U$ *open neighborhood* of $x$, there exists $n$ such that $U_n^x \subset U$

### A2 (second-countable)
A **second-countable** [[topological space]] $(X, \mathcal{T})$, (i.e. $A_2$*-space*) is one with a *countable basis* i.e. 
- there exists a *countable* family of [[open set|open sets]] $\{U_1, U_2,...\}$ which form a [[topological basis|base]] of [[topology]] $\mathcal{T}$
	- i.e. any *open set* is a union of (not necessarily finite) sets in the family (countable subcollection)
